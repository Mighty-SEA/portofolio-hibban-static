# Design Direction — Hibban Habiburrahman Portfolio

Dial: ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2

## Identity & Mood
- **Style:** Neobrutalism (bold 2px–3px solid `#000` borders, crisp hard offset shadows `shadow-neo`, high-contrast typography).
- **Audience:** Software engineering recruiters, technical leads, IT managers, and freelance clients in Indonesia & globally.
- **Voice:** Direct, technical, grounded in real production apps and hardware/network troubleshooting.

## Color Palette
- **Core Neutrals:** White (`#FFFFFF`), Pure Black (`#000000`), Dark Slate (`#1F2937`).
- **Themes (User-Selectable):**
  - `theme-sunny`: Primary Yellow (`#FFD23F`), Secondary Blue/Cyan, Accent Green.
  - `theme-mint`: Primary Sky Blue (`#38BDF8`).
  - `theme-cyber`: Primary Soft Violet (`#E9D5FF`).
  - `theme-sunset`: Primary Warm Orange (`#FB923C`).
- **Rule:** High-contrast black text on light surfaces and primary fills to maintain WCAG AA readability across all four themes.

## Typography
- **Display / Headings:** `Outfit` & `Space Grotesk` (bold uppercase hierarchy for section badges and project titles).
- **Body:** `Inter` (clean sans-serif for case studies, descriptions, and timelines).
- **Technical Labels:** Monospace for stack tags, metrics, and contact coordinates.

## Layout & Liveliness
- **Rhythm:** Asymmetric Hero (copy + interactive PuzzleAvatar) -> Marquee tech banner -> 2-column Project Case Studies -> Sticky-title Timeline Experience -> 2-column Contact & Direct Form -> Footer.
- **Motion:** Scroll-progress timeline bar, card hover offset compression (`translate-x/y` into hard shadow), and reveal transitions.
